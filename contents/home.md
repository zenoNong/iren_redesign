<strong>ɪreːn 🌊</strong> (Interdisciplinary Research on Ethical & Equitable NLP for under-resourced Languages) is a research collaboration dedicated to serving under-resourced languages: languages with little parallel data, few speakers available for paid annotation, writing systems that mainstream models do not cover, and communities whose data deserves careful governance.

We build and evaluate language technology for these languages, study how such systems should be measured, and create open tools that let speakers judge the output themselves. Our work so far includes translation and evaluation for Indic languages such as Manipuri (Meiteilon), written in both Bengali and Meitei Mayek script, and we welcome work on any under-resourced language.

ɪreːn brings together researchers from Indian Central Universities, IITs, IIITs and NITs, along with international labs/institutions worldwide.
